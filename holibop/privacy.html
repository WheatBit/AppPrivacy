HoliBop! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version: HoliBop! collects nothing, from anyone. Every countdown is worked out on your device from today's date and the holiday rules built into the app, and works without the internet. The only thing the app downloads is holiday pictures, and it sends nothing about you to get them. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is ever sent to us. The only purchase is an optional tip, paid through Apple.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the HoliBop! holiday countdown app and its widgets for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by Methodical Enterprises LLC ("we," "us"). Both versions work the same way, and where they differ, this page says so.

What we collect

Nothing. HoliBop! has no user accounts. We never receive your name, your age, which holidays you count down to, or any information about how you use the app.

The internet: holiday pictures only

The holidays, their dates and the wording are built into the app. The countdown is worked out on your device from its own date and time zone, and the widget's number changes at midnight by itself, without the app being opened and without the internet.

Pictures for the first 13 holidays (Halloween through St. Patrick's Day) are built into the app too. The scenes and characters for the other holidays are downloaded once, the first time you open or add one of them, from our own picture storage at art.methodical-ep.com (hosted by Cloudflare), and then kept on your device. Until they arrive, that holiday shows a plain look. Only the app downloads; the widget never does.

These downloads are plain requests for picture files. They send nothing about you: no account, no identifier, and nothing about which countdowns you have. Like any website, the server that sends the pictures sees your device's internet address for the moment it takes to send them. We don't keep records of who downloads pictures, and we never use them to identify or track anyone.

What stays on your device

When you set up a widget, you choose which countdown it shows: a holiday, "Next holiday", "Newest countdown", or one of your own. Your phone keeps that choice with the widget, as it does for any widget.

In the app, you can choose how each countdown looks and add your own countdowns, each with a name and a date, such as a birthday. HoliBop! saves these on your device, where its widget can read them. None of this ever leaves your device except through backups that you control: your own iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad, or your Google backup on Android.

To choose a good moment to ask for a rating, HoliBop! also counts on your device how many days you've opened it. Apple (on iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (on Android) shows the rating prompt; we never see the count or your answer.

If you ask Siri or Google Assistant about a countdown, for example "Talk to HoliBop" and then "How many days till Christmas?", your words are handled by Apple or Google as for any voice request, under their own privacy policies. HoliBop! works out the answer on your device and sends nothing to us.

Permissions

HoliBop! asks you for no permissions. It does not use your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, or health data. On Android, it lists two that need no asking: internet access, for the holiday pictures, and starting after a restart, so the widget keeps changing at midnight.

Notifications

HoliBop! sends no notifications. There is no push server, and nothing is sent to us.

The optional tip

On iPhone and iPad, starting with version 1.3, HoliBop! is still free, and Settings has a Support HoliBop! page where you can leave an optional tip. A tip unlocks nothing. It is paid through Apple's own In-App Purchase, under Apple's privacy policy. HoliBop! never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details, keeps no record of the tip, and sends nothing to us. The Android version has no purchases.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

HoliBop! contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The only outside code is Google Play's own rating prompt on Android. We do not build a profile of you and do not track you across apps or websites. There are no subscriptions or ads, and the only purchase is the optional tip above.

Children's privacy

HoliBop! is made for families, and it is safe for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, children included. It never asks for a person's name, email, age, or location, has no way to chat or share with other people, and uses the internet only to download holiday pictures, sending nothing about the child. There is nothing for a child to give away. A tip can only be left through Apple's own purchase screen, where Family Sharing's Ask to Buy applies.

Keeping and deleting your data

The only things HoliBop! keeps are your widget choices, the looks you picked, your own countdowns, that count of days, and which version's What's New card you've seen. You can delete your own countdowns in the app, removing a widget removes its choice, and deleting the app removes everything, downloaded pictures included. We hold nothing to delete, because we never had it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and post the new version here.
