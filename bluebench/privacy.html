BlueBench — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version: BlueBench runs entirely on your device. It doesn't collect your data, doesn't track you, and doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else. It makes no internet connections at all. There are no accounts and no servers.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the BlueBench app for iPhone and iPad, published by Methodical Enterprises LLC ("we," "us"). BlueBench is a Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) scanner and explorer. It also has a section for Ambiq devices, which does not yet update firmware or stream audio. If we add those features, we'll update this policy first.

What we collect

Nothing. BlueBench has no user accounts and no backend servers, and it contains no code that connects to the internet. We never receive information about you, the Bluetooth devices around you, or how you use the app.

Bluetooth access

BlueBench asks permission to use Bluetooth so it can do its job. When you grant this:

  • BlueBench scans for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices and shows what they advertise, such as their names and signal strength.
  • It connects only to the devices you choose, so you can see their services and characteristics, read and write values, and subscribe to updates from them.
  • All of this happens directly between your device and the Bluetooth device. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party over the internet.
  • You can revoke Bluetooth access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.

What stays on your device

  • Scan results are kept in memory only while the app is running. They are not saved.
  • The activity log (scans, connections, values read and written, and any markers or notes you add) is also kept in memory only, and is gone when the app is closed. BlueBench also writes these log lines to the iOS system log on your device, where Apple's developer tools can show them.
  • Test recording sessions are saved only if you start one. Each saved session holds its name, times, summary numbers, and the log lines captured while it ran. Sessions stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Settings, such as the scan interval, are stored on your device.

This information never leaves your device except through features of iOS that you control, such as your own iCloud device backup, or when you deliberately export or share a file yourself.

Exporting and sharing

You can export the activity log as a text or CSV file, and a saved session as a CSV file of its log lines and a JSON file of its summary. You can also copy a value to the clipboard. These files are created on your device and go wherever you send them using the iOS share sheet. We are not involved and receive no copy.

Microphone, files, and Apple Intelligence

BlueBench does not use the microphone and never asks for microphone access. It does not download, open, or send firmware files. It does not use Apple Intelligence. If a future version adds any of these, we'll update this policy first.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

BlueBench contains no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

BlueBench does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in control. You can delete any saved test session in the app. Deleting the BlueBench app removes all of its data from your device. We hold nothing to delete, because we never had it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and post the new version here.
