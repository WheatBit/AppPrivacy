GiftList — Terms of Use

Version 1 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version: GiftList is for planning gifts with people you know. Be kind on the lists you share. If someone isn't, you can block them or report them, and a list's owner can remove people. We answer every report within 2 business days.

Who GiftList is for

GiftList helps you keep wish lists and plan gifts, and share a list with people you invite: family, friends, a group you buy for. A shared list is only visible to the people who were invited to it.

What isn't allowed

On shared lists, in wishes, notes and suggestions, and in your sharing name, you may not post or send:

  • harassment, bullying, threats or hate toward anyone;
  • sexual content, and anything that sexualizes minors (which we report to the authorities);
  • violence, or content that encourages hurting people or yourself;
  • illegal goods or services;
  • spam, scams or links meant to trick people;
  • pretending to be someone else.

What you can do

  • Block someone on a shared list. Their wishes, suggestions and notes disappear from your phone. They aren't told. You can unblock them any time.
  • Report a person, a wish, or a whole list. The report is an email you send from your own mail app, so you see exactly what it says.
  • Leave a list at any time, or stop sharing a list you made.
  • The owner of a list decides who is invited to it.

What we do

Shared lists are sealed on your phone before they leave it, so we can't read them. That means we act on what a report shows us. We answer every report within 2 business days. We may ask a list's owner to remove someone, and we report illegal content to the authorities. GiftList also checks words on your phone, never sending them to us, and asks before you share something that may break these rules.

Your content

Your lists, notes and wishes are yours. You're responsible for what you share, and you give the people you invite permission to see it in GiftList.

Privacy

How GiftList handles your information is in the privacy policy.

Changes

If we change these terms, we'll update the version and date at the top, and GiftList will ask you to agree again the next time you share.
