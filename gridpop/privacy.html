Block Party Blast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version: Block Party Blast collects nothing, from anyone. Every game is played and saved on your device, and works without the internet. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is ever sent to us. Purchases go through Apple, and scores go to Apple's Game Center only if you are signed in to it.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Block Party Blast puzzle game for iPhone and iPad, published by Methodical Enterprises LLC ("we," "us").

What we collect

Nothing. Block Party Blast has no user accounts and no servers. We never receive your name, your age, your scores, or any information about how you play.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, best scores, stars, Daily Puzzle streak, bonus Undos and Continues, theme, sound setting and stats are saved on your device. They leave it only through backups that you control, such as your own iCloud backup.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Block Party Blast posts your best scores, Daily Puzzle results and achievements to Apple's Game Center, so they can appear on its leaderboards. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy, and you can sign out of Game Center in Settings at any time. If you aren't signed in, nothing is sent.

Purchases

The game is free. Block Party Pro and the puzzle packs are one-time purchases made through Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Block Party Blast never sees your name, your Apple Account or your payment details; it only learns from Apple which items this device's Apple Account owns, so they stay unlocked. Purchases can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Permissions and notifications

Block Party Blast asks you for no permissions. It does not use your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, or health data. It sends no notifications.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

Block Party Blast contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not build a profile of you and do not track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Block Party Blast is safe for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, children included, and has no way to chat or share with other people. Purchases can only be made on Apple's own payment screen, where a parent's approval applies (Ask to Buy).

Keeping and deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device. Game Center data is Apple's to manage. We hold nothing to delete, because we never had it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and post the new version here.
